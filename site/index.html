El Niño flood response

El Niño years bring heavy rain to Uruguay and rivers such as the Uruguay, Negro, Yí, Cuareim and Olimar overflow. Zoom in to a riverside town, then see which ground a river rise would cover, which public buildings stay dry as refuges and a road route that avoids flooded streets.

3.0 m

Planning aid, not an official plan: in an emergency follow SINAE and the departmental Cecoed. Simple model: water level spreads from rivers over terrain (NASA SRTM, ~30 m); it ignores levees, drains and one-way streets. Refuges are public buildings from OpenStreetMap, not confirmed shelters.

About

NISAR radar layers are products derived from real NISAR GCOV backscatter (HH, HV in dB) cut from NASA Earthdata: precomputed for Uruguay and processed on demand for any other area by the local server, scripts/serve.py. Alerts are candidates from simple rules, not confirmed events. The arrows skip dates without tiles or whose pass covers less than 95% of the box (still listed in the menu).

Made by Rafael Rodriguez Sanz, Montevideo, Uruguay, for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, with AI assistance (Claude Code). Data: NISAR GCOV (NASA-ISRO) via NASA Earthdata / ASF DAAC, NASA CMR, NASA GIBS, NASA SRTM, Open-Meteo. Basemap, towns, roads and buildings © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
