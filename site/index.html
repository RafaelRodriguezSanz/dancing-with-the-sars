About the data

Calibrated layer: real NISAR GCOV backscatter cut from NASA Earthdata for the Santa Lucía box (red = HH, green = HV, blue = HH−HV, fixed dB scale). GIBS layer: false-colour tiles for any area, not calibrated. Alerts are candidates from simple rules, not confirmed events. The arrows skip dates without tiles or whose pass covers less than 95% of the box (still listed in the menu).

Built with AI assistance (Claude Code). Data: NISAR GCOV (NASA-ISRO) via NASA Earthdata / ASF DAAC, NASA CMR, NASA GIBS (incl. Blue Marble). Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors.
