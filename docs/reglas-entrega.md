# Submission rules (from the official 2026 guide)

Source: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/ (retrieved 2026-11-13).

- **Window:** opens Sat Nov 14, 09:00; closes Sun Nov 15, 23:59, local time of the Local Event (Universal Event: Team Owner's local time). Internal target: submitted Sun 20:00.
- **Demo:** slide deck of at most 7 slides (title and intro included) OR video of 30 s or less. Exceeding the limits = not eligible for Global Judging.
- **Language:** English everywhere. A video may be spoken in another language only with English subtitles. Slides must be in English.
- **Slides must include:** team name, team member names, chosen challenge, image of the project, user experience, how NASA/space-agency data makes it possible.
- **Links:** demo and final project hosted externally (GitHub, YouTube, Drive…), public, no password/permission/registration. Test in a private browser window.
- **Website tip (optional):** request a FortiGuard Web Filter Classification for the Pages site (Company Name: "N/A").
- **Project page:** images JPG/PNG/GIF up to 10 MB; no video or document uploads.
- **Required fields:** project name, high-level summary, demo link, final project link, detailed description, Super Intelligence (SI) use, NASA data, partner data and other references.
- **NASA data:** at least one NASA data source/resource is required for Global Awards.
- **References:** list all data, code, text and images used, even open source.
- **AI ("Super Intelligence") rules:** allowed as a tool; must NOT be used to come up with the idea. Generated content must not use or modify NASA branding (logos, flags, mission identifiers). Images/videos: visible watermark. Audio, code and data: acknowledge in descriptive text and metadata.
- **People:** no images, video, audio or likeness of persons under 18.
- **Submit:** tick "Terms and Conditions" and "Confirm All Work is Original", then "Submit for Judging" (only Rafa). Resubmission allowed until the deadline.
- **Judging criteria:** Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation.
- **Coding before the weekend:** the guide does not say. Until confirmed, phases 0–3 are preparation only.
